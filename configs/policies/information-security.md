# Information Security Policy

Owner: CISO
Approved by: Chief Executive Officer
Last reviewed: 2026-04-02
Version: 3.1

## Purpose

This policy sets the direction for protecting Northwind Labs information and systems.

## Scope

It applies to all employees, contractors and systems that store or process company data.

## Roles and responsibilities

The CISO owns this policy. Managers make sure their teams follow it.

## Policy statements

- Access is granted by role and reviewed quarterly.
- Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
- Security incidents are reported within one hour.

## Exceptions

Exceptions need written approval from the CISO and expire after 12 months.

## Review

The policy is reviewed every year and after major changes.
